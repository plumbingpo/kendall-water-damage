Leaking Pipe Water Damage Help in Kendall

A leaking pipe rarely announces itself. The drip is hidden in a wall cavity, a sink cabinet or beneath the slab, so the first clue tends to be a stain, a spongy patch of floor or an oddly high water bill. By then the materials around the pipe have often been wet for weeks. Our team in Kendall finds how far the moisture has spread and dries all of it, not just the part you can see.

The plumbing repair stops the water, but it does not remove what has already soaked in. Getting it out is the work we do. Our crew maps the damp materials with thermal imaging and moisture meters, opens up no more than necessary, and runs dehumidifiers and air movers until the readings return to normal. A slow leak gives mold plenty of time to begin, so we inspect for growth too and treat any surface it has reached.

Do This First

Before We Arrive

  1. Shut Off the WaterWhen a toilet or sink line is dripping, shut the valve on the supply right at that fixture. When the source is unclear, the house main is the valve to turn off.
  2. Stay Clear of ElectricityWater around a switch, outlet or light fixture means the breaker for that part of the house should go off. Do not touch wet electrical devices or cords.
  3. Read the MeterWith every tap off, look at the meter. If the dial is still moving, a pipe is leaking somewhere you cannot see, possibly under the slab.
  4. Empty and PhotographEmpty the closet or cabinet by the leak, then photograph the pipe, any stains and any warped material before repairs start or items are discarded.
  5. Get a Moisture CheckAsk for meter readings of the area before anyone patches it closed. Drywall can feel dry to the touch while the cavity behind it is still damp.
How We Fix It

Our Leaking Pipe Water Damage Process

1

Find Every Wet Area

Using a thermal camera and moisture meters, our crew checks cabinets, floors and walls and marks each wet spot, even those far from the pipe that water reached by running along the framing.

2

Open What Is Needed

To get at wet cavities we open only specific spots, take out unsalvageable cabinet parts, drywall or insulation, and seal anything moldy in bags so its spores stay out of the rest of the home.

3

Dry the Structure

Air movers and dehumidifiers run until the framing and subfloor reach normal moisture readings, usually 3 to 5 days. Readings are taken at each visit, and the setup is changed as the materials dry.

4

Final Treatment and Records

We apply antimicrobial treatment to affected surfaces and give you moisture logs and photos. Those records show the area was dry before repairs and help with any claim.

Coverage

Insurance and Leaking Pipe Water Damage

A slow leak and a sudden one are not handled the same way by insurance. Coverage is common for accidental, sudden damage, while a leak that ran for months or weeks is usually not covered and is specifically excluded in some policies. An adjuster may look more kindly on a leak hidden from view than on one that could be seen and was left alone. Read your policy and talk to your agent. We document the source of the leak, moisture readings and photos so the adjuster has clear facts.

Why It Happens

How It Usually Starts

  • Tiny pinholes that form as corrosion gradually works through aging copper pipe.
  • A nail or screw driven into a pipe during a remodel or while hanging something on the wall.
  • Pipes in exterior walls or crawl spaces that crack after a freeze and seep once they thaw.
  • High water pressure that stresses joints and valves year after year until one weeps.
  • A P-trap or drain connection under a sink that loosens and drips each time the basin empties.

What Happens if You Wait

Swollen Cabinets

Particleboard cabinet bases under a sink swell and crumble once they absorb water. Swollen board stays swollen, and after a lengthy leak the base often has to be replaced.

Ceiling and Drywall Damage

Leaks over a ceiling or behind a wall surface show as bubbling paint and brown stains. Drywall that stays wet goes soft, sags and may need to be cut out.

Rotted Framing

After weeks of dampness, bottom plates and wall studs start to go soft and rot. Once framing lumber is no longer sound, it must be removed and new wood put in.

Wet Insulation

Insulation in the wall soaks up leak water like a sponge and holds it against the framing. Since a closed wall gives it almost no airflow, soaked insulation is normally removed.
